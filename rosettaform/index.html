Filtering: click a family once to include it, twice to exclude it (NOT), a third time to clear it. With several families included, any shows languages with at least one of them (OR), all only those with every one (AND). Clicking a family tag on a card toggles it the same way. The current view is stored in the URL, so it can be shared.

## Reading

### Papers (chronological)
- [Ricci 1973 – A Constructive Geometry for Computer Graphics](https://doi.org/10.1093/comjnl/16.2.157) – solids as min/max of functions, incl. smooth blends; ancestor of CSG and F-rep
- [Wyvill, McPheeters & Wyvill 1986 – Data Structure for Soft Objects](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01900346) – blobby fields with compact support + polygonizer
- [Lorensen & Cline 1987 – Marching Cubes](https://doi.org/10.1145/37402.37422) – the standard field-to-mesh algorithm
- [Osher & Sethian 1988 – Fronts Propagating with Curvature-Dependent Speed](https://doi.org/10.1016/0021-9991(88)90002-2) – founds the level-set method
- [Perlin & Hoffert 1989 – Hypertexture](https://doi.org/10.1145/74333.74359) – noise-modulated density fields as shape modeling
- [Pasko, Adzhiev, Sourin & Savchenko 1995 – Function Representation in Geometric Modeling](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02464333) – formalizes F-rep / R-functions; theory behind HyperFun
- [Hart 1996 – Sphere Tracing](https://doi.org/10.1007/s003710050084) – Lipschitz-bounded ray marching; basis of every Shadertoy raymarcher
- [Curless & Levoy 1996 – A Volumetric Method for Building Complex Models from Range Images](https://doi.org/10.1145/237170.237269) – truncated SDF fusion (→ KinectFusion, scanning)
- [Bloomenthal (ed.) 1997 – Introduction to Implicit Surfaces](https://www.sciencedirect.com/book/9781558602335/introduction-to-implicit-surfaces) – the textbook
- [Frisken, Perry, Rockwood & Jones 2000 – Adaptively Sampled Distance Fields](https://doi.org/10.1145/344779.344899) – octree-adaptive SDFs for sculpting and fonts
- [Ju, Losasso, Schaefer & Warren 2002 – Dual Contouring of Hermite Data](https://doi.org/10.1145/566654.566586) – sharp-feature-preserving meshing
- [Green 2007 – Improved Alpha-Tested Magnification for Vector Textures and Special Effects](https://doi.org/10.1145/1281500.1281665) – Valve's SDF text; made SDFs standard for fonts/UI

- [Keeter 2020 – Massively Parallel Rendering of Complex Closed-Form Implicit Surfaces](https://doi.org/10.1145/3386569.3392429) – GPU interval arithmetic + tape pruning; behind libfive/Fidget
